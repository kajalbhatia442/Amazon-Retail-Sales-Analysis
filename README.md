# Amazon-Retail-Sales-Analysis 
An exploratory analysis of Amazon retail sales data focused on revenue trends, product categories, brands, and country-level performance. The project summarizes the findings in a dashboard and presentation. 

## Project Goals

- Review revenue trends over time.
- Compare revenue across product categories and brands.
- Examine revenue by country.
- Identify findings that could inform inventory and marketing decisions.

## Key Findings

- **Sales trend:** April 2023 had the highest revenue in the period analyzed.
- **Category performance:** Electronics generated the most revenue among the categories reviewed.
- **Brand performance:** Fit Life generated the most revenue among the brands reviewed.
- **Country performance:** The United States generated the most revenue among the countries reviewed.
- **Overall pattern:** Revenue was concentrated in a small number of high-performing categories, brands, products, and regions. Returned orders and lower-performing regions may be worth further investigation.

These are descriptive findings from this project dataset. The analysis does not establish why these results occurred; for example, seasonal demand or promotions would need separate evidence to confirm.

## Tools and Methods

- **Tools:**  the tools you used Excel, Power BI, Tableau,  or Python 

- **Methods:** Revenue trend analysis and comparisons by category, brand, and country. 

## Recommendations

- Maintain sufficient inventory for strong-selling categories and brands.
- Focus promotions on products with demonstrated demand.
- Review returned orders for possible product or fulfillment issues.
- Investigate lower-revenue regions and test targeted promotions or availability improvements.

These are suggested actions based on the analysis, not outcomes measured by the project.
